            Certified teachers, still learning

            All our teachers hold the Iyengar Yoga Teachers Certification Mark, and keep training throughout their teaching lives.

              Thinking of becoming a teacher?

              Iyengar teacher training follows the national pathway of Iyengar Yoga (UK). Several of our senior teachers are approved mentors, independently of the Institute, and selected workshops welcome trainees and mentees to observe adjustments.

              The teaching pathway

              A charity, run by its members

              Teachers and students raised the money for our first building with jumble sales, coffee mornings and sponsored headstands. Mr Iyengar came to open it in 1984, and donated the proceeds of his Barbican demonstration to the new Institute.

              We still reinvest every penny in making Iyengar yoga accessible to everyone, with free tasters, concessions and a welcome for every age, body and background.
