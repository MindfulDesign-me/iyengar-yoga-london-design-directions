            Lineage

            1. 1971

              A method takes root

              Through the 1970s, more than 150 teachers train under Silva Mehta at the Paddington Institute, waiting for Mr Iyengar’s annual visit to learn whether they may teach.

            2. 1983

              A home of our own

              Teachers and students raise the deposit with jumble sales, coffee mornings and sponsored headstands, and buy a former artist’s studio in Maida Vale.

            3. 1984

              Guruji opens the Institute

              Mr Iyengar gives a demonstration at the Barbican and donates the proceeds to the new Institute. The mortgage is paid off within five years.

            4. 1994

              The building we practise in today

              After a dedication puja led by Mr Iyengar in 1993, the new building opens on 14 May 1994, ten years to the day.

            5. 2024

              Forty years

              Abhijata Iyengar visits Maida Vale to celebrate the Institute’s fortieth anniversary.

            6. 2026

              Pune in Maida Vale

              A week of workshops and celebration with Pune teachers Sushama Chitale and Devki Desai.

            Certified teachers

            Every teacher here holds the Iyengar Yoga Teachers Certification Mark, and keeps studying throughout their teaching life.

            Becoming a teacher

            Training follows the national pathway of Iyengar Yoga (UK). Several senior teachers here are approved mentors, independently of the Institute, and selected workshops welcome trainees and mentees to observe adjustments.
