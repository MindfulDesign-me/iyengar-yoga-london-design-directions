Maida Vale, London · Since 1984

The original London home of Iyengar yoga.

B.K.S. Iyengar helped found this centre and taught here. Forty years on we teach his method as he did: with safety, detail, precision and alignment, for complete beginners and long-standing practitioners alike.

A charity, run by its members, for everyone who wants to practise.

Iyengar Yoga London is the city’s original Iyengar yoga centre. Teachers and students raised the money for the first building through jumble sales, coffee mornings and sponsored headstands, and in May 1984 Mr Iyengar came to London to open it, giving a demonstration at the Barbican and donating the proceeds to the new Institute.

Today we offer more than forty classes a week in the studio and online, specialist and therapeutic classes, and workshops with our own teachers and visiting senior teachers from Pune and around the world. As a not-for-profit charity, we reinvest every penny into making Iyengar yoga accessible to everyone, with free taster classes, concessions and an inclusion policy that welcomes all ages, bodies and backgrounds.

Why Iyengar yoga

Iyengar yoga asks you to perform each posture with care, and then to stay in it. Postures are held for longer, so muscles relax and lengthen while awareness is raised. Rather than leaving you to copy the teacher, an Iyengar teacher explains, demonstrates and corrects.

  1. PrecisionClear demonstration and explanation, and individual correction when you need it.
  2. PropsBelts, blocks, blankets and the rope wall make every posture safe and accessible, whatever your body.
  3. SequencingA varied, systematic programme that builds strength, flexibility, stamina, concentration and calm.
  4. ProgressionA clear path from Beginners to General and Intermediate, with pranayama introduced as your practice matures.

            A committed team of certified teachers

            Every teacher here holds the Iyengar Yoga Teachers Certification Mark, a sign of training, clarity and continuing study. Senior teachers such as Judy Smith bring more than thirty years of teaching to every class.

              Becoming a teacher

              Teacher training in the Iyengar method follows the national pathway set by Iyengar Yoga (UK). Several of our senior teachers are approved mentors, independently of the Institute, and selected workshops welcome trainee teachers and mentees to observe adjustments. We also invest in the ongoing development of our own teaching team.
